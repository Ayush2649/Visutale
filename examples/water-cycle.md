# How the water cycle works

Prototype input notes for exercising storyboard planning and review. These are development notes, not a claim that source research or fact-checking has been performed.

- The water cycle describes the movement of water between Earth's surface and the atmosphere.
- Sunlight supplies energy that drives evaporation from bodies of water.
- Plants also release water vapour through transpiration.
- Water vapour cools and condenses into droplets that form clouds.
- Water returns to Earth's surface as precipitation, including rain and snow.
- Water may flow over land, soak into soil, or collect in bodies of water.
